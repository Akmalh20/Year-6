Discover Radical 火 (Fire) & 米 (Rice)

In Chinese characters, radicals (部首 - bùshǒu) give clues to the word's meaning. Let's explore how 火 and 米 build words!

火
火 Pinyin: huǒ

Fire Radical (火 / 灬)

Variant Form: 灬 (Four Dots at Bottom)

Represents heat, light, cooking, burning, or high energy. When placed at the bottom, it often transforms into four dots (灬)!

米
米 Pinyin: mǐ

Rice Radical (米)

Meaning: Grains & Food Culture

Represents grains, crops, sticky or powdery substances, and food. It is essential in Chinese agricultural history!

The Story of Radical 米 (Mǐ)

米

Evolution of Rice Grains

Ancient Chinese people drew grains of rice scattered around a stem. Today, it forms characters related to food, agriculture, and sweet foods.
